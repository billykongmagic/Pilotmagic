About Pilot Magic

Creating Extraordinary Magic in Macau

"Every performance is more than a show — it's an immersive experience that connects audiences and communicates brand values."

Founded in 1997, Pilot Magic Co., Ltd. has upheld a commitment to professionalism, creativity and artistry — delivering premium magic entertainment for every type of event across Macau.

With over 20 years of experience, we have proudly served government bodies, international luxury brands, five-star hotel resorts and private high-end clients — tailoring every performance to the event theme, venue and audience.
