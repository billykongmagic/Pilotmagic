Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Answers to questions parents ask us most

【About Performance & Age】

What age group is the magic show best suited for?

Our show is custom-designed for children and families. We **highly recommend it for kids aged 4 and above**, as they can fully grasp and enjoy the interactive nature of magic. It is also designed to be highly entertaining for parents and grandparents alike!

When is the best time to schedule the magic show during the party?

We usually recommend scheduling the magic performance **30 to 45 minutes after the party starts**. By then, most of your guests will have arrived, settled in, and built up the excitement — making it the absolute golden window to captivate their attention and elevate the atmosphere.

How are the cake cutting and balloon twisting handled in the 1hr & 1.5hr packages?

Using our popular **1-Hour Package** as an example:

  • First 35 mins: The main highly interactive magic show, highlighting the birthday child as our "Junior Wizard".
  • Next 10 mins: The magician hosts the cake-cutting ceremony, leading everyone in singing the birthday song and arranging group photos.
  • Final 15 mins: The magician twists colorful custom balloon gifts for all child attendees to take home.

【About Venue & Preparation】

Are there specific requirements for the venue? (House, clubhouse, restaurant, or outdoors)?

Our mini theater experience is **extremely flexible and can be performed indoors or outdoors**. We only require space for the children and guests to sit and view the show comfortably.

*Note: Outdoor performances or remote venues may involve additional travel or setup charges. Please consult us during booking.*

Do parents need to prepare equipment like sound systems or tables?

Parents **do not need to worry about any technical details**! We bring all necessary sound equipment and specialized props. We only suggest leaving a small, clear performance space (approx. 2x2 meters) for the best setup.

【About Language, Fees, and Booking】

What languages are available for the performance?

Our performances are **primarily hosted in Cantonese**. If your party requires a full **English or Mandarin** show, we offer this service with a surcharge of MOP $500. Please let us know upon booking.

What is the "Magic Gift Souvenir Add-on" and when should I order?

Each MOP $30 gift set includes an interactive, easy-to-learn magic prop. The magician will demonstrate the trick live and teach the kids how to do it. Since props are prepared per event, **please place your order at least 2 weeks before the party**.

Are there hidden costs? What do you need to check availability?

Our package rates are **strictly flat and fully transparent — absolutely no hidden fees**. To check availability for your date, please contact us via WhatsApp or WeChat with the following details:

  1. Event Date & Preferred Time
  2. Venue Location (Indoor/Outdoor)
  3. Estimated Number of Children & Guests
  4. Age of the Birthday Child
